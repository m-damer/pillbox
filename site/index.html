Pill Box

A free medicine reminder for Android with large, readable text. It works offline, has no account and no ads, and keeps your data on your phone.

Download for Android

Privacy policy

Your medicine data stays on your phone. There is no account and no server. Nothing is sent unless you choose to contact the developer.

Privacy policy · Terms of use

This app helps you remember your medicine. It does not replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist.
